What Step 6 builds on (Onboarding.py)
==========================================================================
- speed = meters_second(rpm): motor RPM -> m/s (12/41 gear ratio, 0.2 m
  wheel radius). This is WHEEL speed.
- moving = speed > 0.1
- Laps (Step 4): Lap 1 = 20-68 s, Lap 2 = 95-133 s. "1.5 laps": Lap 2
  never crosses back over the start line; it ends when the car stops.
- find_periods(condition): turns a True/False mask into (starts, ends)
  lists. Merges gaps under 0.1 s, drops periods 0.7 s or shorter. Works
  on the full `time` column, so masks must be full length.
- time_in_lap(starts, ends, lap_start, lap_end): clips periods to a lap
  window, PRINTS them, returns only the TOTAL seconds. It does not return
  the clipped periods, which Step 6 will need.
- Step 5 coasting (the one to use, NOT Step 3's `coasting` variable):
    coasting_5 = (pedal == 0) & (brake <= 375) & moving
    coast_starts, coast_ends = find_periods(coasting_5)
  "Not gaining speed" was deliberately left out: coasting is about the
  driver (no throttle, no brake), and a car coasting downhill can speed up.
- Step 5 coasting periods clipped to the laps (37.18 s / 32.83 s):
    Lap 1: 24.63-41.18, 42.88-46.41, 48.63-49.53, 51.80-68.00
    Lap 2: 99.33-103.03, 103.83-132.96
  coast_starts/coast_ends ALSO contain periods outside the laps (before
  20 s, between 68 and 95 s, after 133 s). Step 6 says "from all laps",
  so only the in-lap parts should go forward.

Things to think about in Step 6 (raise as questions, don't decide)
==========================================================================
- Order of filters: remove rows under 5 m/s first, then drop pieces
  shorter than 1 s? Removing slow rows can cut a long coast short or
  split it, so the 1 s check should probably come after the speed cut.
- What "anything below a second" applies to: whole coasting periods, or
  the pieces left after the 5 m/s cut.
- Expected: Lap 1's 48.63-49.53 s coast (0.90 s) is dropped. Speed in it
  is around 4 m/s anyway.
- The output should be data rows (time + speed), not just totals,
  because Step 7 computes dv/dt on it and fits dv/dt vs v^2. Keeping each
  coast as a separate segment helps: dv/dt must not be computed across
  the gap between two segments.
- Lap 2's gap at 103.03-103.83 s is the driver tapping the pedal; it is
  correctly not coasting and splits that coast into two pieces.

==========================================================================
Data quirks (all verified)
==========================================================================
- SME_TRQSPD_Speed updates only every ~2nd row; forward-fill repeats the
  old value. Row-to-row dv/dt is mostly 0 or noise. Use a window: the
  project uses .diff(42) (~0.5 s) for acceleration. Matters for Step 7,
  where projects.md says "change in speed between consecutive readings"
  - that will be very noisy here.
- .diff(n) looks BACKWARD, so labels lag ~0.25-0.5 s; a period computed
  inside a lap loses its first n rows. Step 5 computes on the full run
  first, then cuts to laps.
- Wheel speed vs car speed: at hard throttle peaks (~51.6 s, ~98-99 s)
  there is likely wheelspin/driveline wiggle. Not an issue during coasts.
- Brake is never pressed during either lap (voltage max 372 < 375).
  ETC_STATUS_BRAKELIGHT reads 1 for 95% of the run - unreliable, don't use.
- IMU VDM_X_AXIS_ACCELERATION points BACKWARDS (positive = slowing) and
  has a ~+0.317 m/s^2 offset (likely mount tilt; +/-0.1 m/s^2 between
  coasts). Useful as a cross-check for Step 7 decelerations.
- VDM_GPS_SPEED is already in m/s, NOT mph (projects.md is wrong).
  Updates every ~0.24 s and lags motor speed by ~0.9 s.
- GPS altitude is whole meters with ~16 updates per lap: too coarse to
  measure slope. Each lap drives out and back, so coasts happen in both
  directions; slope effects largely cancel if both are used (Step 7).
