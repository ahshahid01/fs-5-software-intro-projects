Step 2 - Speed of the car at 10 s

Answer: 0 m/s (the car is stopped).

Speed is in m/s, converted from SME_TRQSPD_Speed (motor RPM):
   speed = RPM * gear ratio (12/41) * 2 * pi * wheel radius (0.2 m) / 60

How I found it:
   - On the speed vs. time graph, the line is flat at 0 around 10 s.
   - Checked the data directly: the row closest to 10 s (t = 9.998 s,
     row 839) has SME_TRQSPD_Speed = 0 RPM -> 0 m/s.
   - It isn't a forward-fill artifact: the raw (not forward-filled) data
     has 84 real readings between 9.5 and 10.5 s, and all of them are 0.

Context:
   - The car last moved at ~6.77 s (a short roll at the start, max
     ~0.8 m/s) and doesn't move again until ~19.86 s, right before the
     Lap 1 launch. So 10 s is in the middle of a ~13 s stop.
   - GPS agrees: VDM_GPS_SPEED is 0.01 - 0.04 m/s between 9 and 11 s,
     which is just GPS noise for a parked car. (VDM_GPS_SPEED is already
     in m/s, not mph like projects.md says - see step5 notes.)
